Companion app for Nuvio

Set up Nuvio once. Then never again.

Numax builds a working Nuvio profile from scratch — API keys, streams, metadata, add-ons — then copies that setup onto every other profile you own and backs it up to your own Google Drive.

How it works

Four steps from a blank Nuvio account to a setup that follows you everywhere.

typical flow
01Link your accountSign in with Google, then add your Nuvio email and password.
02Run the wizardKeys, streams and metadata written straight into the profile you pick.
03Sync it acrossPush that setup to your other profiles instead of redoing it by hand.
04Back it upSave the lot to your own Google Drive so it's recoverable.

Browser extension for Nuvio

Your Nuvio setup, everywhere you use it.

Numax saves your addons, plugins, collections, settings, and watch progress from one Nuvio profile and applies them to any other — instantly, without redoing the setup by hand every time.

Add to Chrome See how it works
Numax is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Nuvio.

What it does

core features

Save a profile as a template

Capture a profile's addons, plugins, collections, and settings as a reusable template you can apply anywhere.

Apply across profiles

Push a saved template to any other profile on your account — no manual re-adding, no re-configuring.

Carry over watch history & progress

Bring watch history and in-progress titles along with the rest of a profile's configuration, if you choose to.

Back up to your own Google Drive

Optionally save templates to a Drive folder Numax creates for itself — nothing else in your Drive is touched.

TYPICAL FLOW

Save profile → template
Pick target profile
Apply template
Optional: back up to Drive

Data & access

short version

Numax reads and writes your own Nuvio profile data using your existing Nuvio session — the same access you already have logged into nuvio.tv directly. Google Drive backup is opt-in and limited to files Numax creates itself. Numax doesn't run its own servers and doesn't sell or share your data. Full details, including a permission-by-permission breakdown, are in the privacy policy.
